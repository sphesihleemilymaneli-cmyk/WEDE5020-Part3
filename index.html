Organisational Overview

Formally registered in 2024, our grassroots work commenced in 2021 through targeted interventions addressing urgent community needs across the Intsika Yethu Municipality.

Our Mission

To empower Cofimvaba communities through targeted programmes in education, health, and social development, creating sustainable pathways to opportunity and self-reliance.

Our Vision

A thriving, resilient Eastern Cape where every community member has the tools, support, and opportunity to realize their full potential and contribute to collective prosperity.

Deep Embeddedness

Direct access to 3 local schools and hundreds of families through trusted relationships.

Proven Capability

Consistent delivery of annual programmes with clear, measurable outcomes since 2021.

Strategic Approach

Successful collaboration with government departments and private sector leaders.

Sustainable Models

Community-owned initiatives designed specifically for long-term lasting impact.
